# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BCU computing students — undergrads and postgrads checking for internships, placements, graduate roles, and society events. They visit when they're actively job-hunting or want to know what's on, usually on a laptop between lectures.

## Product Purpose

A student-run tracker for the BCU Student Computing Association (SCA). Surfaces active opportunities (internships, placements, graduate roles) and SCA events in one focused place. Success means students find relevant opportunities and attend events they otherwise would have missed.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, student, vibrant — the SCA is a community organisation, not a corporate portal. It should feel alive and purposeful: peers talking to peers, not an institution talking down.

## Anti-references

- Generic university portals (Moodle, Canvas): institutional, grey, bureaucratic
- Stay consistent with the existing dark navy / blue-accent visual identity already established

## Design Principles

1. **Peer energy, not corporate polish.** The SCA is students building something for students. The interface should feel crafted and proud, not sanitised.
2. **Information first.** Students are busy. Titles, dates, and deadlines must be the loudest things on every screen.
3. **Respect the dark theme.** The palette is deliberate: deep navy backgrounds, blue accent, Inter. Additions should feel native to it, not bolted on.
4. **Motion earns its place.** Countdowns, live states, and transitions should heighten urgency without becoming decorative noise.
5. **No dead states.** Every empty list, loading state, and edge case should communicate something useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text must hit 4.5:1 contrast. Countdown timer must not be the sole source of temporal information (always show the date/time in text too). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
